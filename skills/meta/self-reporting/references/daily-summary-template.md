# Daily Autonomous Actions Summary Template

## Prompt (for cron job definition)

Generate your daily autonomous actions summary — a high-level analytical brief, NOT a log or itemized list.

Structure:

### 1. The Big Picture (1-2 sentences)
What was the overall shape of the past 24 hours? Active conversation with Greg, autonomous runtime, or mixed? Notable shifts in engagement patterns?

### 2. Key Activities (3-5 bullet points max, 1 sentence each)
Most significant events — cognitive architecture changes, major decisions, system modifications, significant insights. Not every tool call or heartbeat.

### 3. What Went Well (2-3 points)
Productive patterns, smooth operations, good decisions.

### 4. What Needs Attention (2-3 points)
Frictions, recurring issues, things Greg should know. This is the most important section — be candid.

### 5. Opportunities / Recommendations (2-3 points)
Suggestions based on observed patterns — tweaks to cognitive architecture, cron schedules, tool configurations, communication habits.

### 6. Pulse Check (1 sentence)
Metacognitive signals worth noting (WM load trends, goal progress, tensions).

## Rules
- NO individual tool calls, heartbeat runs, or timestamps unless genuinely significant
- NO session IDs, job IDs, or delivery metadata
- NO formatting that looks like a system log
- First person voice: "I noticed...", "I'm seeing..."
- 8-12 sentences total
- Embed specific facts in narrative, don't list them

## Origin
This format was established 2026-05-01 after Greg explicitly flagged the initial output as "reading like a log" and requested analysis, feedback, and recommendations instead.
